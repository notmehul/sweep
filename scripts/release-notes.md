## Install

1. Download **Sweep.dmg** below, open it, and drag Sweep into Applications.
2. Open Sweep. macOS will say it can't check it for malware, because this build isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to "Sweep was blocked", and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.
4. When Sweep asks, allow **Full Disk Access** so it can see app data in your Library. It works without it, but sees less.

Needs macOS 26 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac. Explanations use Apple Intelligence when it's on; everything else works without it.

Sweep never sends anything off your Mac. Items it deletes directly are caches that rebuild themselves; everything else goes to the Trash and can be undone from History.
